About this model

A demonstration of a model under development, built to the Site Energy Model design spec v0.3. Every figure is produced by that model from the assumptions listed under Assumptions, several of which are still open. Nothing here is a quotation, an offer, or advice on a specific site, and the reference case is illustrative rather than a real project.

This model is not a professional engineering deliverable and does not constitute engineering services. Where a project requires drawings sealed by a professional engineer, the model identifies that requirement; it does not satisfy it.
