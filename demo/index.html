About this page

This is a demonstration of a model under development, built to the Site Energy Model design spec v0.3. Every figure on it is produced by that model from the assumptions listed under Assumptions, several of which are still open. Nothing here is a quotation, an offer, or advice on a specific property, and the reference site is illustrative rather than a real customer.
