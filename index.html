The Challenge

EV Charging is
Complicated.
It Doesn't
Have to Be.

Property owners know they need EV charging. But navigating the electrical engineering, equipment selection, network management, and ongoing maintenance is a full-time job — one you didn't sign up for.

Most properties delay for years because the path forward isn't clear. That's the gap Carolina Recharge was built to fill.

  • 01

    It's a Different World

    Load calculations, NEC compliance, utility interconnection — EV charging sits at the intersection of electrical engineering and property management. It's a specialized discipline, and most property teams are already running at full capacity.

  • 02

    Technology Moves Fast

    Which charger standard? Which network platform? Wrong choices today become expensive problems tomorrow. Keeping up requires constant attention that most property teams can't afford.

  • 03

    Maintenance Never Ends

    EV chargers need software updates, hardware servicing, user support, and network monitoring. It's ongoing infrastructure — not a one-time install.

Getting Started

From Assessment
to Online,
Step by Step

We've streamlined every step so your property can go from zero to fully operational EV charging infrastructure without disrupting your day-to-day operations. Our team manages the entire project.

01

Site Assessment

Our electrical engineering team visits your property, evaluates panel capacity, maps parking areas, and analyzes load requirements. You'll receive a comprehensive technical report with everything needed to make an informed decision.

02

Solution Design & Proposal

We design the optimal charging configuration for your property — charger count, placement, output levels, and network platform. You review a clear proposal with line-item costs before committing to anything.

03

Installation & Commissioning

We coordinate licensed electricians, oversee infrastructure installation, manage permits and inspections, and commission the charging network. You check in at milestones — we handle the rest.

04

Ongoing Management

Once live, your chargers are monitored around the clock from our network operations center. Software updates are deployed remotely. Maintenance is proactive. Tenants have direct access to our support team. Your only job is collecting the amenity value.
